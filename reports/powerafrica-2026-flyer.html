Student Competition 2026
Sovereign Minigrid Electrification & Smart Street-EMS Challenge

Moving beyond standard cloud-centric hackathons, this competition is anchored around the open-source Street-EMS energy management platform. Grounded in the spirit of IEEE Smart Village technology workshops, participants tackle real-world rural minigrid architectures combining physical power engineering with business-case and shipping sensitivity.

The Workshop Track

"Make, Code, Integrate" Format

Delivered across three mentoring-driven sessions during the conference week, this track prepares in-country engineers and student teams to design, assemble, and program resilient minigrid interfaces.

  • 1

    MAKE — Build the Physical EMS Controller

    Assemble a fully functional Street-EMS controller from PCB components. Learn assembly, testing, and hardware debug processes.

  • 2

    INTEGRATE — Cabinet Wiring & Multi-Tenant Metering

    Mount the controller inside a 3-phase Street-EMS reference cabinet with DIN rail breakers, contactors, and multi-tenant metering variants.

  • 3

    CODE — Cloud vs. Cloudless Aggregation Challenge

    Network Street-EMS under alternative cloud and cloudless protocols including DLMS/COSEM, the EnAccess REST API, and OpenEMS frameworks.

Core Architecture Challenge

First-Kilometer Grid Distributed Intelligence

We introduce a truly cloudless, sovereign village electrification architecture option based on Street-EMS embedded LV Feeder distributed intelligence.

Shifting the LV feeder from the traditional "last kilometer" to the "first kilometer" of community-owned, villager-empowered resilient systems.

Key Design Criteria: Measure everywhere, policy-based session admission, telecom-like P2P asset transfer control planes, and transmit-only actionable telemetry.

In-Country Startups Track & Free Trade Logistics

Startups and engineers will gain critical insights into leveraging recent cross-border African Free Trade Agreements (AfCFTA). We introduce proper usage of the Harmonized System (HS) shipping codes for modules and PCB import, assembly, test, programming, repair, and export—enabling commercial viability.

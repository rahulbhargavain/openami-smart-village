2. Corrected Fabrication Dimensions

To achieve the 450mm x 300mm concrete finish with 12mm plywood, use these Outer Dimensions (OD) for your steel frames:

  • Long Span (OD): 474mm
  • Short Span (OD): 324mm

[!IMPORTANT] Double Angle Base

At the 150mm and 325mm levels, bolt two MS angles back-to-back or side-by-side to act as a single reinforced beam. This prevents the steel from yielding under the 58.6 kN/m² of extreme hydrostatic pressure exerted by the wet concrete at the bottom of the formwork.

4. Bar-by-Bar Cutting Strategy (Minimal Waste Optimization)

Follow this layout to pre-cut your 18-foot MS angles with absolute minimal metal wastage:

  • Bar 1: Cut two Vertical Corners ($2 \times 2440\text{mm}$). Waste: 606mm.
  • Bar 2: Cut two Vertical Corners ($2 \times 2440\text{mm}$). Waste: 606mm.
  • Bar 3: Cut 9 Long Yokes ($550\text{mm}$) + 1 Short Yoke ($400\text{mm}$). Waste: 136mm.
  • Bar 4: Cut 9 Long Yokes ($550\text{mm}$) + 1 Short Yoke ($400\text{mm}$). Waste: 136mm.
  • Bar 5: Cut 4 Long Yokes ($550\text{mm}$) + 8 Short Yokes ($400\text{mm}$). Waste: 86mm.
  • Bar 6: Cut 12 Remaining Short Yokes ($400\text{mm}$). Waste: 686mm (Save this clean remnant to use for structural ground kickers).

5. Assembly & Fabrication Notes

  • Bolt Grade & Toughness: Use M12 Grade 8.8 high-tensile bolts for the bottom three yokes (where hydrostatic pressure is maximum). Standard M10 bolts are sufficient for the upper sections from Yoke 4 and above. Ensure the bolts used at the double-angle levels are long enough to securely bind both structural flanges together.
  • Plumb & Alignment Check: Use your remaining angle pieces from Bar 6 as diagonal "kickers" (braces) anchored to the ground on all four sides to keep the 8-foot frame perfectly vertical and plumb during the concrete pour.
  • Form Slurry Sealant: Since you are using relatively thin 12mm plywood, ensure the corners where the 324mm panels meet the 474mm panels are tightly clamped and bolted to the vertical angles to prevent grout slurry leakage, which causes aggregate honeycombing in the concrete.

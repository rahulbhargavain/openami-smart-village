# Initiatives
<img src="/media/home/screenshot_2024-07-25_at_3.32.03 pm.png" class="wiki-image" alt="Embedded Image">IEEE Smart Village (ISV) has supported a large number of projects that have been deployed around the world, which can be explored on our interactive [Projects Map](https://overview-solutions.github.io/RemoteMonitorMap/) imaged to the right.

Generally, projects receive support ranging from $10,000 to $200,000 USD. These go through the [Project Development Committee](/wiki/wg/pdc) and their respective Regional Working Groups, and usually include the criteria that a majority of the recipient team needs to be personally based in the area the grant is for, and there needs to be some level of Educational Impact and/or Entrepreneurial Impact associated with the energy system.

See the [Projects Template](/wiki/playground/template_projects) for an outline of how we are thinking about featuring projects.
